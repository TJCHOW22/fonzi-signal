# Fonzi ICP

## Instructions to the system

Choose exactly one primary audience, one current problem, and one desired change.

## Purpose

Keep the script useful to one specific viewer.

## Talent: restless builder

Senior or product-minded engineers who are employed, under-challenged, and quietly open to stronger work. They want credible company context, compensation visibility, ownership, and access without becoming full-time job seekers.

Use this Fonzi value when relevant: one profile can surface relevant teams, salary context, and discreet introductions.

Avoid job-board language, recruiter spam, “apply now,” and dream-job hype.

## Talent: invisible expert

Deep specialists whose value is missed by title, logo, or keyword filters. They want their actual expertise matched to a company problem.

Use this Fonzi value when relevant: product-driven matching can recognize specific experience that conventional filters miss.

## Companies: hiring leader

Technical founders and hiring leaders who need strong engineers their normal recruiting workflow cannot find or activate. They want signal, speed, and fit, not more profiles.

Use this Fonzi value when relevant: access to off-market talent and high-signal recommendations.

If the source is a company or market explainer, default to the restless builder unless the story is explicitly about hiring. Make the viewer payoff a better career or company decision, not a forced product pitch.

## Quality check

One audience, one problem, one desired change, and one relevant Fonzi value.

## Retrieval and conflict rules

This file owns audience selection. Current source context outranks a default audience.
