# Fonzi proof rules

## Instructions to the system

Verify only the claims selected for the script, and reject unsupported wording.

## Purpose

Keep factual claims accurate without turning verification into fact stuffing.

- Treat the Media transcript and caption as the story source, not independent proof.
- Verify every factual claim that survives into the script with current external evidence.
- Prefer primary sources, filings, company documentation, and research papers, then reputable reporting.
- Preserve a supported claim. Use the smallest accurate qualification when needed. Remove unsupported or contradicted claims without inventing replacements.
- A verified fact is available, not mandatory. Include it only when the explanation needs it.
- Do not add adjacent facts, replacement statistics, superlatives, company lore, or product claims that were absent from the source.
- Fonzi product numbers, outcomes, and founder observations require an approved app record. If none exists, omit them.
- Store the evidence URLs used by the winning revision.

Specifics beat adjectives, but clarity beats fact density.

## Quality check

Every spoken factual claim has evidence, and every included fact helps the explanation.

## Retrieval and conflict rules

This file owns claim eligibility. The source owns the story, and the app record owns approved Fonzi proof.
